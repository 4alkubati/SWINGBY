## 1. A money guard that was written, tested, and never merged

> **RESOLVED 2026-08-12** — branch `fix/unassigned-completion-guard`.
>
> The hole was confirmed against production before anything was written:
> `SELECT (employee_id IS NULL), status, payment_status, count(*) FROM bookings`
> returned **248** rows at `completed` + `fully_released` with **no assignee**,
> and **254 of 263** bookings unassigned overall. Unassigned is the *default*
> state, not an edge case. Of the 248: 204 have no `payments` row (demo seed),
> **19 are backed by a real Stripe PaymentIntent** ($5,557.50 released, sandbox
> keys), 15 are cash.
>
> **The premise in the table below was out of date, and it changed the fix.**
> `complete_booking` no longer releases escrow — that moved to the 24h approval
> window on 2026-07-31 (`bookings.py:1136`, "NO MONEY MOVES HERE"). Money now
> leaves via `approvals.release()`, reached from client-approve
> (`bookings.py:1299`), `settle_if_due`, and `settle_due`. **None of the three
> checks `employee_id`.**
>
> The guard still went in `complete_booking`, for a *new* reason: it is the only
> caller of `start_approval_window`, and nothing else writes
> `payment_status='awaiting_approval'` — a real chokepoint. Putting it in
> `release()` would have been wrong: `settle_if_due` swallows every exception, so
> raising there converts a leak into a **permanent trap**.
>
> **Ordering caught by the smoke test, not by review:** the first draft placed
> the guard before the capture check, which turned the existing 409 "this
> booking has not been paid" into a 400 "assign someone". `tools/e2e_smoke.py`
> failed on it. The guard now sits *after* `assert_releasable`, so the payment
> fact — the more specific one — still wins, and the auto-assign never mutates a
> booking that was going to be refused anyway. A regression test pins the
> ordering.
>
> Also corrected while here: `_is_provider` in `proof_of_work.py:117` compares
> an employee against `booking.employee_id`, so on an unassigned booking
> *employees* are locked out of proof-of-work while the *owner* passes via
> `business_id`. That is an access asymmetry, not trapped money — the release
> paths never consulted `employee_id` at all. It resolves itself once bookings
> carry a real assignee.
>
> `e2e_smoke.py` now asserts the guard rather than routing around it: unassigned
> → 400, then assign → complete → 200. **ALL PASS.** Full backend suite 1043
> passed / 1 skipped; ruff + black clean.

## 3. `expo-splash-screen` is not installed

The white-screen fix (`App.js`, 2026-08-12) stops the app rendering `null`, so a
stalled font load now shows a dark branded spinner instead of a white void. It
does **not** close the underlying gap: nothing holds the native splash across the

--dev-client`, open the build against it, read the error. Left for the debugging
session Kira planned for when they are home.
